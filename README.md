# 📊 ITI Statistics Case Study  — The Placement Report

## 📌 Project Overview

A collaborative case study completed as part of the ITI Introduction to Statistics course.

The project analyzes graduate placement data, lab task completion times, and helpdesk ticket records to explore statistical distributions, evaluate assumptions, and support evidence-based conclusions.

## 🎯 My Contribution

I was responsible for **Part E: Interviews and Offers (Binomial Distribution)** and **Part F: Helpdesk Tickets (Poisson Distribution)**.

### Part E — Interviews and Offers

* Evaluated the assumptions required for applying the binomial distribution.
* Estimated the overall offer rate from interview and offer data.
* Calculated probabilities for different offer outcomes.
* Compared theoretical predictions with observed graduate outcomes.
* Examined the limitations of assuming a constant probability of receiving an offer.

### Part F — Helpdesk Tickets

* Analyzed daily helpdesk ticket counts.
* Compared the mean and variance to assess the suitability of a Poisson model.
* Calculated probabilities for different daily ticket volumes.
* Compared observed ticket frequencies with theoretical expectations.
* Considered the implications of the findings for helpdesk staffing decisions.
